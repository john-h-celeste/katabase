Project Blank was a videogame created by Nothing Inc. in October 1983. It sold 532K copies in the West and 42K copies in Japan. It was
a financial failure, but garnered the attention of Louis Gerald-Redcliffe, whom created his own spinoff, Project Empty, in September 1997. His
game was a commercial success, selling 2.7M copies in the West alone, and revolutionized the way video games told stories in a new 3D environment.
